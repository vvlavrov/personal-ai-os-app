Privacy policy — Personal AI OS (Max Personal Assistant)

Effective date: 2026-10-07

  • Who uses the app: only its owner. It is a private, single-user project and is not offered to the public.
  • What data is accessed: only data in the owner's own Google account that the owner explicitly authorizes (for example Calendar events and files in a dedicated Google Drive backup folder).
  • How it is used: to provide personal-assistant features to the owner and to store encrypted backups of the owner's own server in the owner's own Google Drive.
  • Storage: on the owner's private server and in the owner's own Google account. Backups are encrypted before upload.
  • Sharing: no data is sold, shared with or transferred to third parties.
  • Revocation: access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Contact: the support e-mail address shown on the Google consent screen.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
